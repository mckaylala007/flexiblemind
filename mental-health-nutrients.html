A periodic table for staying okay

Mental health isn't one big thing to fix. It's a set of nutrients — and you can be low on just one.

You already know how this works with food: you don't treat every hunger the same. Some things keep you alive, some things make life good, and some things are poison. Your mind works the same way. Click any nutrient below to see what its deficiency feels like — and how to replete it.

Anti-nutrients

Deficiencies aren't the whole story. Some things actively deplete you — or block the good stuff from absorbing at all. Every toxin has an antidote.

Bitterness
Unforgiveness, jealousy, envy — something curdled toward another person. Replaying old conversations, carrying the same anger for years. Gossip is bitterness leaking out loud.
Antidote: Forgiveness — including forgiving yourself.
Comparison
The robber of joy and gratitude. Constant competition, measuring your life against someone else's — and taking what you have for granted while you do it.
Antidote: Gratitude — the robber can't take what you're actively counting.
All-or-nothing thinking
He's either a good guy or an evil guy. A perfect day or a ruined one. All in, or giving up entirely — the same swing that drives binge cycles. When life has many greys.
Antidote: Flexibility — learning to think in greys.
Perfectionism
Up till 3am making it perfect. Holding perfection as the highest standard — a form of extreme thinking that keeps you stagnant, anguished, and miserable to be around.
Antidote: Compromise — judge perfectionism as imperfect, and hold compromise in higher regard.
The sob story loop
Over-investing in your own suffering story instead of the mindsets and actions that change it. It can become an addiction — and it keeps you alone in it.
Antidote: Action + Giving Back — problem-solving over endless complaining; attention off yourself is the fastest exit.
People-pleasing
The doormat pattern. Everyone's yes but your own, and their opinions running your decisions.
Antidote: Authenticity — with boundaries and learning your attachment style as the tools.
Insecurity
Shrinking. Second-guessing your worth in every room you walk into.
Antidote: Confidence — built through self-love and small brave actions.
Fear of rejection
Self-doubt doing the rejecting for them. Not sending it, not saying it, not trying — pre-rejecting yourself first.
Antidote: Faith + Boldness — inner confidence over imagined verdicts.
Pride that keeps you stuck
Too proud to ask for help, to apologize, to admit the plan isn't working. Vanity guarding a door you need to walk through.
Antidote: Humility — asking for help is strength, not defeat.
Forever-doom thinking
Extreme, end-of-the-world vibes. Blowing things out of proportion just because they feel loud.
Antidote: Perspective — the step back. Ask: is this 100% true?
Worry
Rehearsing futures that haven't happened. The mind spinning on what you can't control tonight.
Antidote: Faith — inner confidence. The opposite of worry.
Rest guilt
Lying down but never landing. The constant hum of "I should be doing something." Deep down, that's not resting.
Antidote: Healing your relationship with rest.
Isolation
Some seasons of solitude are necessary — but too long, and it cements. Your nervous system adapts until even sending a text feels jarring.
Antidote: Support, repleted slowly — new habits, inches out of the comfort zone at a time.

Tools

Tools aren't nutrients — they're how you replete them. The same tool shows up behind different doors. When you keep meeting one, that's the table telling you it's load-bearing for you.

Boundaries

Nobody wakes up feeling "low on boundaries." What you feel is drained, resentful, overextended. Boundaries are the skill running underneath — how you reclaim the time and energy the other nutrients need.
